4. ABITTA — MODULE: BILLING ENGINE & TRANSACTION INTEGRITY (ACID)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Primary Responsibilities:
  - Designed the billing data structures: 'SaleItem' and 'TransactionRecord'.
  - Engineered the multi-item billing cart mechanism: adding medicines, validating stock
    limits, verifying non-expired status, and calculating running totals.
  - Implemented transactional ACID guarantees in DatabaseHelper.saveTransaction():
    - Disabling auto-commit (conn.setAutoCommit(false))
    - Inserting transaction headers and line-item details
    - Decrementing stock quantities in the database
    - Committing atomically (conn.commit()) or rolling back on error (conn.rollback())
  - Created automatic sequential invoice number generation (INV-1000, INV-1001, etc.).

* OOP & Technical Concepts Demonstrated:
  - Class Composition / Aggregation (TransactionRecord composed of List<SaleItem>)
  - Transactional Integrity & Rollbacks (ACID principles in JDBC)
  - Business Rules Enforcement (Preventing sale of expired or insufficient stock)
  - String Formatting & Mathematical Calculations
